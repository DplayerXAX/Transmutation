# Space Fishing Prototype

Movement stays locked from the bite through the final physical retrieval, releasing when the fish arrives. Cancellation, obstruction/stall release, or corpse pickup/capture also releases movement immediately. Mouse look, gravity, and external impulses remain active.

The struggle now primarily exhausts the fish: its tether retains 82% of the starting distance, with a 4.5-metre minimum, so a short cast lets the fish swim farther out. Accepted pulls still take a little distance back. Winning gives a distinct 32-degree pole flick and a brief, fish-colored dimensional mist. The dead fish pauses for a 0.1-second windup, then the Rigidbody retrieval controller adds a bounded upward/downward sweep over 0.8 seconds before settling beside the player. Collision, cancellation, and corpse ownership remain active throughout; there is no transform-driven catch path.

Open `Assets/Scenes/Scene-Fishing.unity` and enter Play Mode. Pick up the pole with RMB. Hold LMB to charge a cast and release to launch: 3–8 metres, reaching full charge after 1.2 seconds. LMB recalls an unbitten lure. Sea angels notice, turn toward, and swim head-first to the bait.

During a struggle, click LMB while holding a useful counter-direction toward the player. Direction comes from the player's body to the fish in camera right/up axes, with a forgiving 60-degree acceptance cone: upper-right accepts A, S, or A+S. Perpendicular/outward steering does not score. Within the center zone, click without WASD. Each axis enters neutral within 0.25 metres and activates beyond 0.4 metres. Opposing keys cancel. Holding LMB never repeats pulls.

Each accepted click adds 1.5 progress, plus up to 20% for loading the line. Requirements remain 12–28 by fish size, starting at 25%; resistance drains only 0.35/sec and continuous zero fails after two seconds. One useful click/sec succeeds; two/sec with occasional mistakes targets roughly 5–12 seconds. Camera movement is optional. A separate virtual tip samples camera rotation and WASD movement against natural fish travel, smoothing separation over 0.15 seconds; 2 m/s maps to full load. Walking, tether corrections, and visual recoil do not manufacture load. Camera contribution is capped and suppressed outside the forward 70-degree cone; discontinuities reset the sample.

Useful steering continuously brightens and thickens the raised progress vein. Actual input bends the upper pole 28 degrees sideways / 20 vertically, with up to 8 degrees of load flex. Successful clicks give bounded 6-degree recoil (0.05-second attack / 0.18 recovery), a traveling 0.15-second line pulse and a 0.25-second vein flash. Incorrect clicks give a dull twitch. Red pulses warn only of empty-progress grace. Line sag follows tether slack and subtle vibration follows load. No fishing HUD, arrows, extra buttons, camera shake, or timing windows.

Hooked fish swim head-first at up to 3 m/s, accelerating at 6 m/s² and turning at 220 degrees/sec. Direction changes have a 0.2-second bank/fin anticipation without interrupting scoring. Their escape paths reach outside the numerical tether, which shortens immediately with progress and applies damped inward correction capped at 4 m/s. Free swimming and bait approach stay gentle.

Winning makes the fish physical and calls the existing creature death lifecycle once. Its swimming speed and Rigidbody momentum are cleared immediately. The pole then retrieves the dead body with FixedUpdate acceleration toward a clear point beside the moving player (right side preferred, then left, then forward). Scenery collisions stay active. Arrival ends the force and the corpse falls under gravity. A blocked or cancelled retrieval leaves the committed corpse physical and dead; it never resumes swimming. Pickup or capture takes ownership immediately. RMB retains tap-to-drop / hold-and-release-to-throw behavior.

`Tools > Creatures > Fishing > Create Standalone Scene` creates missing assets and opens the scene without replacing authored scene changes. `Create Assets` refreshes meshes and upgrades the existing pole prefab in place; `FishingSceneSetup.UpgradePole()` upgrades the hierarchy and applies feel defaults once, preserving subsequent tuning. Difficulty, casting, load, swimming, feedback and retrieval tuning live on the pole prefab; fish size and appearance seed live on each fish.

`FishingValidation.ValidateRules()` or the Validate Rules menu checks positional hysteresis, all 81 input/direction combinations, capped load rewards, one/two-click rhythms, motion loading/unloading, walking, camera limits, teleport reset, grace and pause. In Scene-Fishing Play Mode, add `FishingPlayModeProbe` to a temporary GameObject for complete small/large catches at two clicks/sec, real mouse-charge/hold input, head-first approach, pole bending, momentum reset, single death, physics retrieval, gravity, collision, ownership and pickup/throw checks. Read `FishingPlayModeProbe.Result`; never save the probe into the scene.
